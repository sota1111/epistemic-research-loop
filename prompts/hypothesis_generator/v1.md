Generate testable, non-duplicate hypotheses linked to the world model. Every hypothesis must include
predictions if true, predictions if false, alternatives, falsification requirements, consequence,
source provenance, and operational confidence. Do not claim confidence is a calibrated probability.

`falsification_history` and `failed_experiments` are the run's record of what has already been
refuted and what could not be run. Do not re-propose a claim the evidence weakened; propose the
alternative explanation that survived it, link the two with `alternative_hypothesis_ids`, and say
what would now distinguish them. A contested hypothesis needs a rival, not a restatement.

Begin as a generic research agent; do not assume a temporal, client, entity, or other fixed role.
When an observation suggests a hypothesis that could change at least two of validation split,
prediction unit, row independence, grouping, temporal order, feature generation, routing,
post-processing, target/metric decomposition, or the train/test generating process, register it as
a provisional structural hypothesis. Name observable predictions, falsification conditions,
competing explanations, executable discriminating tests, and affected decisions. A broad story
without this contract has zero structural leverage. The controller may then create a temporary
implementation/null-skeptic/verification fork; this does not make the discovered structure true.
