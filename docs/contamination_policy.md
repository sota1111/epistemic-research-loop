# Contamination policy

Strict historical benchmarks prohibit Kaggle discussions, writeups, code/notebooks/topics,
competition-specific GitHub repositories or articles, and post-competition solutions. Official
competition description/rules/metric/schema and general material published before the competition
are allowed with content hashes and retrieval provenance.

Literature queries use generic domain concepts and never include the competition slug. Experiment
workers have networking disabled; only a source-policy proxy may retrieve approved literature.
